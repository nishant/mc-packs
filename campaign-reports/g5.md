## Scenario fixes (the code was right, the test was wrong)

- `loadArea` returns `{ top }` as a Block: `top + 1` threw in bram-migrate, two-towns, mayor-add, capital-perf and life (1f0e0ee, 8e47b03).
- Two dumps or censuses in the same tick picked up the earlier answer: build-resume compared Oakfield with itself, and residents checked the stray before its census ran. Helpers now wait a tick (b4318591, d41df08).
- Slopes reached below bedrock (y -77) or outside the loaded circle, so every `/fill` failed. A `/fill` that changes nothing fails too. Grass buried under the hill decays to dirt mid-test (1b133b6, 4cb9e6f, 991af19).
- town-houses read the moved families' looks in the same tick the profession event fired (probe: events apply a tick later) (3218083).
- town-two-towns tapped inside Townsfolk's 10-tick tap cooldown (c33aaca).
- town-resident-poi: villagers never set `occupied_bit`, so the control could never pass and the gate proved nothing. It now uses `/setblock` and checks `isSleeping` on the bed (94a5896).
- town-hillside: an enderman took a grass block outside the regions during the 20-minute run; mob spawning is now off. Spot waits are up to 10 minutes (1818a5b, 178f1e1).
- town-capital-perf: it can't use `--no-relay`, because the setup reads dumps from chat. Ten simulated players hold the world's 10 ticking areas, so they are freed for the transients. It stands by the pens before counting animals. `--arg npc=1` adds a one-NPC baseline (a0f6e22, 06f5217, f7e623b).

## Decisions

- **D72**: a resident claiming a bed is detected by `isSleeping` on the bed, since villagers never set `occupied_bit`.
- **D73: residents default to Enabled.** The evidence:
  - No resident slept in 5 runs, while an ordinary villager slept in the same bed.
  - Residents never work (0 of 48 crops taken).
  - Strays are brought back.
  - When a town is removed, its residents live on untagged and its record is deleted (town-residents).
  - The world cap held at 20 (capital-perf).
  - No villager was born in any run.
  - Items: a moved family drops what it carries (`dropCarried`); a removed town kills no villager.
- **D74**: residents get the jobless schedule and lose the work groups. They keep their profession look.
- **D75**: the census leash is 32 blocks, skipped while a player is within 16.
- **D76**: pen animals that vanish come back while a player is by the pen; killed ones never do (no free food).
- **D77**: the scan is paced for QuickJS. Performance comes before UX: on town-hillside's terraces the levels took **81 / 510 / 0 / 308 s** (it was 35 / 248 / 0 / 157 s at workTick 400). Flat ground: about 1 minute. The docs say "a minute or two, up to about ten on a steep hillside".
- The shareables override (stopping residents picking up food) was tried and dropped: the bread probe showed no pickup even without it, so the change was unproven. An empty `items` array is also invalid content.
- I did not touch CLAUDE.md. The `villager_v2` line in "Vanilla entity overrides" is still owed by the lead (WORKING.md lists it).

## newsLines (player-facing, plain ASCII)

- Every town now has a mayor of its own. Add a town and its mayor (like Mayor Alda of Riverside) is waiting at the town's center, ready to take deliveries for that town's projects.
- Mayor Bram is still the realm's main mayor and keeps his town. Every other town gets its own mayor the next time someone visits it.
- Towns now pick their own building spots on natural ground and level it gently (never more than 3 blocks), and they never touch anything you built, placed or planted.
- As towns grow they build houses, lay grass paths to the center, light them with lantern posts and keep a garden pen with sheep and chickens.
- Villagers now move into growing towns: 2 by the well, then a family for each house. They never take your bed, never harvest your crops and wander home when they stray.
- Operators: /realm:town -> Mayor moves the town's mayor to where you stand or renames them.

## Risks

- **Spot picking on steep ground is slow**: up to about 8.5 minutes for a Village's 6 projects (D77). This is the cost of keeping a scanning tick near 1 ms.
- **Breeding**: the jobless schedule's wander and home events add `make_and_receive_love` back. Breeding needs claimable beds, and residents claim none; no villager was born in any run, but there was no long soak.
- **Removed towns**: residents of a removed town stay as ordinary villagers that keep the resident group (no POI claims). Each add-and-remove cycle of a town can leave up to 10 villagers.
- **Pen top-up**: animals that wandered more than about 40 blocks from the pen while still loaded could be duplicated (the count uses every loaded animal with the tag, so a far one still counts). In practice the fence keeps them in.
- **Timing interference**: the scan-40 transient overlapped with a local tsc and git work for about 1 minute; its 1.66 ms may be slightly high.
- Remove spot can still overwrite a player's block whose kind the build uses (unchanged from g5-towns; containers spill).
