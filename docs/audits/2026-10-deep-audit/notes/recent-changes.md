## Verdict
1. The publishes are sound: master == published byte for byte for all 74 packs, sha256 values are correct, bundles are current, and C7 is clean. #91 did what it said.
2. The one real risk is the cross-page refill (#90): it scans every page's structure snapshot on each used-up stack (high perf), matches looser than Hotbar Refill itself (wrong rockets mid-flight), and lacks the death guard every other stash path has.
3. The quest box (#88) is protocol-correct but unverified on a client (C1). Its size and placement need a real screen check, and notes now blank it briefly.
4. The one-line coordinates HUD with the party and stats extras gets too wide for phones and needs a wrap rule.
5. Prefs locks (#89) are correct and well split. The rest are low-cost polish items (nagging popup, max-blocks default, choice migration).
